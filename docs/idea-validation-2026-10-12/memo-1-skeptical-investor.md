# Skeptical Investor

I've heard this pitch before. In the evidence pack, the people who tried it either ran out of money or sold out, and the incumbent already gives the product away free.

**1. It has been done, and the deaths are on record.** Mycroft ran out of money in early 2023, could not deliver Mark II rewards, and dumped units on eBay at $499. That is inside this idea's price band (https://www.opensourceforu.com/2023/02/open-source-virtual-assistant-project-mycroft-is-cash-strapped/). Sonos bought Snips, the privacy-focused local voice platform, and shut its console within a week (https://venturebeat.com/business/sonos-acquires-privacy-conscious-ai-startup-snips-for-37-5-million). The closest small-vendor analogue, Useful Sensors' $299 offline-LLM box, raised only $46,041 (https://www.crowdsupply.com/useful-sensors/ai-in-a-box). The researchers found **no commercially successful small-vendor local-LLM home appliance** (INSUFFICIENT DATA flagged in the pack). The "what's different now" answer is "small LLMs exist." But Home Assistant already plugs in local LLMs through Ollama (https://www.home-assistant.io/blog/2025/09/11/ai-in-home-assistant). So that is not a difference.

**2. This is a feature, not a business.** Home Assistant is free, has 699,274 opt-in active installs (https://analytics.home-assistant.io/), and offers the same "local, no subscription" claim (https://www.home-assistant.io/voice-pe). Green plus Voice PE costs about $268. That sets the price anchor that $300–600 has to beat. Nabu Casa, with about 30 staff and the whole community behind it, is profitable only by combining hardware with **subscriptions** (https://www.builtinnyc.com/company/nabu-casa). This plan drops the recurring half and keeps the hard half.

**3. The unit economics are thin before you count anything hard.** Wholesale for a 3550H/32GB unit is $274 at 1–99 units (https://www.globalsources.com/product/mini-pc_1224414489f.htm). IDC expects DRAM-driven price hikes of 15–20% into 2027 (https://www.techradar.com/pro/experts-warn-pc-prices-could-rise-even-more-in-2026-as-ram-costs-set-to-soar-higher). At a $450 retail price the gross spread is about $176. Amazon's 8% fee takes about $36 of that (https://www.repricer.com/?p=1593). Smart-home returns run up to about 14.9%, and 68% of those are "no trouble found" (https://eightx.co/blog/average-electronics-return-rate-benchmarks; https://www.corso.com/post-purchase-resource-center/electronics-returns-what-every-seller-needs-to-know). Support, FCC responsibility, liability insurance and app-store fees are all INSUFFICIENT DATA. Even if the full $176 spread survived, $25k net would take more than 140 hand-assembled units a year (arithmetic). Tindie's median is **4 orders per product** (https://blog.tindie.com/?p=8731). CAC: INSUFFICIENT DATA. LTV equals one unit's margin, by design.

**4. "Free updates forever" with one-time revenue is the Futurehome failure pattern.** Futurehome went bankrupt and then imposed a mandatory subscription, or users lost control of their devices (https://www.techdirt.com/?p=511300). With no recurring revenue, every unit sold adds an unfunded support and OTA obligation.

**5. There is no moat, and the product doesn't work yet.** "Single C# stack" is invisible to buyers; the pack found no evidence anyone cares. On the target box, the time to first token is about 135 seconds (panel context). STT/TTS, the home-automation integrations, the mobile app and OTA updates don't exist. Gemma 4 needs a custom prompt renderer. Telegram is a hosted messaging service, so it conflicts with "cloud-free" (SPECULATION about how buyers will see it, but the conflict itself is structural).

**6. Demand is unproven, and the old data points away from it.** Surveys from 2017–2022 put price above privacy: 60% of non-owners said speakers were too expensive (https://feeds2.benzinga.com/tech/17/10/10194340/the-top-reason-some-wont-buy-a-smart-speaker-price-not-privacy). People keep buying despite privacy worries (https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2019-09-05/users-worry-about-smart-speaker-privacy-keep-on-buying-them). The pack has no 2025–26 evidence that "local AI" drives purchases.

**Steel-man of the bull case:** Umbrel Home sells a one-time-price box with free updates at $699 (https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/umbrel-announces-new-umbrel-home-plug-and-play-server). HA's non-technical household members do reject its complexity (https://community.home-assistant.io/t/a-room-first-home-assistant-frontend-for-the-rest-of-the-household/1020977). Umbrel's sales are INSUFFICIENT DATA, though, and that gap is HA's to close, not a solo founder's.

At realistic medians this is beer money with an open-ended support liability.

SEVERITY: FATAL
